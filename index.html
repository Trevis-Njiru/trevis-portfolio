What I do (and I love doing).

I'm Trevis Njiru. I have a deep passion for creating visuals and truly enjoy doing graphic design. I love transforming abstract ideas into something solid and meaningful. I'm eager to learn many things and always look forward to exploring and improving my creative design sense.

Alongside the design work, I run social media management and communications for brands and community projects — planning what gets posted, writing the words that go with it, and keeping the whole thing feeling like one voice instead of a scramble of graphics. It's the part of the job that happens after the file is exported: making sure the design actually lands with the people it was made for.

Let's collaborate and turn your ideas into striking visuals that truly resonate.
